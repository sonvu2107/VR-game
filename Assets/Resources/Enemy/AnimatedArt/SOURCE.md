Enemy animation art provenance
===============================

Imported 2026-10-19 for the VR-game enemy animation upgrade. These are pixel-art sprite sheets loaded at runtime by EnemyRuntimeSpriteAnimation. Sprite sheets are retained as source textures; individual animation frames are sliced in Unity.

- MonsterSkeleton.png, MonsterWitch.png, MonsterZombie.png — “Animated Monsters”, stealthix, CC0. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/animated-monsters
- Bat.png — “Bat animations”, Vander96, CC0. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/bat-animations
- UndeadKing.png — “Undead King”, Reemax and artisticdude, CC0. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/undead-king
- SkeletonArcher.png — “Skeleton Archer”, Wolfgang Wozniak (Ouren), CC BY 3.0. Attribution: artwork by Wolfgang Wozniak (Ouren). Source: https://opengameart.org/content/skeleton-archer
- VampireIdle.png, VampireWalk.png, VampireRun.png, VampireAttack.png, VampireHurt.png, VampireDeath.png — “Vampire 4-direction Pixel Sprite”, CraftPix, OGA-BY 3.0. Attribution: CraftPix.net. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/vampire-4-direction-pixel-sprite

No paid asset was used. License and attribution notes are kept here with the imported files.
