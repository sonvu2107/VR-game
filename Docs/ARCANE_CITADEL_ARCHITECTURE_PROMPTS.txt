Draw an EXACT 4 by 4 grid of sixteen square floor texture tiles covering the entire square canvas edge to edge, equal cells, NO gutters, NO margins, NO grid overlays, NO labels or text. All tiles are flat overhead squares, NOT isometric, NOT floating cubes, no raised bevel around individual cell perimeter. Texture-style game art with crisp pixel clusters, consistent light from upper left, low contrast so characters stand out. Do not use any existing game tile art. Each cell is fully opaque.

Row 2: four worn stone variants: hairline cracks, chipped slab, sparse moss in grooves, faint old scratches.

All sixteen cells have the same base stone hue and scale. Plain tiles should have matching edge colors suitable for repetition; avoid black backgrounds and exterior shadows. Actual source art sheet, not a screenshot, no props or walls.
